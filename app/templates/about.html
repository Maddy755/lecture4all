About Lecture4All

Lecture4All is an educational technology system providing cross-lingual semantic retrieval for recorded lectures, connecting Dutch (Nederlands) lecture content to multilingual Indian student audiences.

Source → Target Translation Architecture

Recorded university lectures in Nederlands (Dutch) are transcribed using OpenAI Whisper. The speech transcripts are then translated using neural machine translation models into three target languages: Tamil (தமிழ்), Malayalam (മലയാളം), and Hindi (हिन्दी). Multilingual vector embeddings index the aligned transcript chunks, enabling students to search concepts in their native language and jump directly to the exact video timestamp.
