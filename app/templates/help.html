Help & Guidance

Lecture4All enables you to search across multilingual educational lecture videos using queries in English, Nederlands (Dutch), Tamil (தமிழ்), Malayalam (മലയാളം), or Hindi (हिन्दी), jumping straight to relevant timestamps.

🌐 1. Universal Cross-Lingual Search

Lectures can be in any language (English, Dutch, Tamil, Malayalam, or Hindi). The semantic search engine understands meaning cross-lingually.

🎯 2. Choosing Search Language

In the header, select your search language: English, Nederlands, தமிழ், മലയാളം, or हिन्दी. The search suggestions and voice input align automatically.

⏱️ 3. Jumping to Timestamps

Click on any matching result or specific timestamp badge (e.g. ⏱ 04:15) to open the player and immediately start playback at the exact explanation segment.

🎙️ 4. Voice Search

Click the microphone icon next to the search bar. The speech recognizer listens in your selected language (English, Dutch, Tamil, Malayalam, or Hindi).

For questions, feedback, or support regarding Lecture4All, reach out to the project team.
