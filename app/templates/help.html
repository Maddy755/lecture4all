Help & Guidance

Lecture4All enables you to search within Dutch educational lecture videos using translated queries in Tamil (தமிழ்), Malayalam (മലയാളം), and Hindi (हिन्दी), jumping straight to relevant timestamps.

🇳🇱 → 🌐 1. Translation Workflow

Lectures originate in Nederlands (Dutch). The system transcribes and translates the content into your selected target language (Tamil, Malayalam, or Hindi).

🎯 2. Choosing Target Language

In the header, select your target language: தமிழ், മലയാളം, or हिन्दी. The search placeholder and sample queries will automatically align with your selection.

⏱️ 3. Jumping to Timestamps

Click on any matching result or specific timestamp badge (e.g. ⏱ 04:15) to open the player and immediately start playback at the exact explanation segment.

🎙️ 4. Voice Search

Click the microphone icon next to the search bar. The speech recognizer listens in your selected target language (Tamil, Malayalam, or Hindi).

For questions, feedback, or support regarding Lecture4All, reach out to the project team.
